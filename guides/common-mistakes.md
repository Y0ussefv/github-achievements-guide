# ⚠️ Common mistakes and troubleshooting

## “I did it, but the badge didn't appear”

| Possible cause | What to check | Status |
| --- | --- | :---: |
| Processing delay | Wait — reports range from minutes to 24 hours. Check in a private window. | 🧪 |
| Achievements hidden | **Settings → Show Achievements on my profile** is checked | ✅ |
| Badge hidden individually | Click the badge on your own profile; unhide it | ✅ |
| Private profile | Achievements are hidden from others on private profiles | ✅ |
| Commit email not linked | `git config user.email` must match an email on your account | ✅ |
| Work in private repositories | Private contributions setting; behaviour per badge is undocumented | ❓ |
| PR not merged | Closed ≠ merged. Check the PR shows **Merged**. | 🧪 |
| PR merged in your fork only | Must be merged into the upstream repository | 🧪 |
| Not enough for the tier | Check the [tier thresholds](../README.md#-tier-thresholds) | 🧪 |
| Profile showed a lower tier | Fixed on 11 Sep 2026 — profiles now show the highest tier ([Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-09-11-profiles-now-show-your-highest-achievement-badge-tier/)) | ✅ |
| All badges vanished at once | Community members have reported temporary platform-wide display issues; wait, then contact GitHub Support | 🧪 |

Sources: [Profile contributions reference](https://docs.github.com/en/account-and-profile/reference/profile-contributions-reference) ·
[Manage visibility settings](https://docs.github.com/en/account-and-profile/how-tos/contribution-settings/manage-visibility-settings-for-private-contributions-and-achievements)

## Mistakes that can hurt your account or reputation

- ❌ **Buying or trading stars/followers.** Inauthentic activity violates the
  [Acceptable Use Policies](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/acceptable-use-policies/github-acceptable-use-policies).
- ❌ **Running “achievement farming” scripts** that mass-create PRs, issues or repos.
- ❌ **Spamming PRs to other people's projects** (e.g. adding your name to a README).
- ❌ **Adding fake co-authors** without their consent.
- ❌ **Bypassing branch protection or reviews** on shared repositories just for YOLO.

## Good habits

- ✅ Use the no-reply email to keep your address private.
- ✅ Keep PRs small and focused.
- ✅ Read each project's `CONTRIBUTING.md`.
- ✅ Treat badges as a by-product of good work.
