## 1. What it represents

Public Sponsor is awarded for sponsoring an open-source developer or organisation
through [GitHub Sponsors](https://docs.github.com/en/sponsors/getting-started-with-github-sponsors/about-github-sponsors).

## 2. Requirements

| Requirement | Status |
| --- | :---: |
| Sponsor a user or organisation through GitHub Sponsors (one-time or monthly) | 🧪 |
| The sponsorship must be **public** (not marked private) | 🧪 |
| One-time badge, no tiers | 🧪 |

## 3. Step by step

1. Think of a maintainer or project you depend on. On their profile or repository, click **♥ Sponsor**.
2. Choose a **monthly** or **one-time** tier that fits your budget.
3. Keep the visibility **public** so the sponsorship appears on your profile.
4. Complete checkout with your own payment method.

Official walkthrough: [Sponsoring an open source contributor](https://docs.github.com/en/sponsors/sponsoring-open-source-contributors/sponsoring-an-open-source-contributor).

To find maintainers of projects you already depend on who accept sponsorship, visit
[github.com/sponsors/explore](https://github.com/sponsors/explore) (requires sign-in).

## 4. Check whether it was awarded

Look for the badge on your profile. Your public sponsorships are also listed on the
**Sponsoring** tab of your profile.

## 5. Restrictions and ethics

- 💸 This is the only achievement that costs money. Sponsor because you want to support someone, not for a badge.
- Cancelling a sponsorship is reported not to remove an earned badge. ❓
- GitHub Sponsors is not available in every country/region — see the official docs.
